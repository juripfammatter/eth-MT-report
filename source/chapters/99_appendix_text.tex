\section{Algorithms}\label{sec:appendix_algorithms}

\Cref{alg:training_loop,alg:random_walk,alg:threshold,alg:mix} summarize the proposed
method in pseudocode. \Cref{alg:training_loop} is the bidirectional training loop that
alternates between exploration on random-walk candidates and exploitation on the
frontier and solved buffers, \Cref{alg:random_walk} expands the frontier by simulating
action-noise random walks from the nearest neighbor of a sampled task-space point,
\Cref{alg:threshold} sorts the collected rollouts into the frontier buffer $F$ and the
solved buffer $S$ based on their return, and \Cref{alg:mix} rebuilds the working set
$\mathcal{I}$ from those two buffers, replaying a fraction $\rho$ of solved states.
