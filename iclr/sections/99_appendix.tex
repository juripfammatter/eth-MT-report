\subsection{Algorithms}\label{sec:appendix_algorithms}

Algorithms~\ref{alg:training_loop}--\ref{alg:mix} summarize BVER in pseudocode.
Each direction keeps the same three sets of states: a frontier buffer $F$ holding the
states whose rollout return falls between $R_{\min}$ and $R_{\max}$, a solved buffer $S$
holding the states with a return above $R_{\max}$, and the mixed set $\mathcal{I}$ from
which the exploitation rollouts are drawn. The superscripts $\text{fwd}$ and
$\text{bwd}$ distinguish the forwards from the backwards expansion; states in the
forwards buffers enter the rollouts as intermediate goals through $\phi$, states in the
backwards buffers as intermediate start states.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:training_loop} is the bidirectional training loop, which collects
exploration rollouts on the random walk candidates and exploitation rollouts on the
mixed set in every iteration and updates a single goal-conditioned policy on both.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:random_walk} expands a frontier by simulating an action-noise random
walk from the nearest neighbor of a sampled task space point,
Algorithm~\ref{alg:threshold} sorts the collected rollouts into $F$ and $S$ by their
return, and Algorithm~\ref{alg:mix} rebuilds $\mathcal{I}$ from the two buffers,
replaying a fraction $\nu$ of solved states.
